-- sponaugle (2 replies) --
I am using IotaWatts to do monitoring of every circuit in my house, and it is a pretty great setup.   I have a reasonable large setup given I have 9 panels to monitor with >120 individual circuits.  I also have it monitoring my 20kw Solar system as well as 42kwh of batteries.
https://community.iotawatt.com/t/sorta-larger-than-average-i...
I have all of the data going into influx, with grafana for display.
During the build of my house I planned for this by adding additional space below the panels: https://www.garagejournal.com/forum/threads/jeffs-mountain-s...
However that was more of a nice to have, not something you have to do.
On the solar side, if you want per panel measurements that is something you can really only do with micro-inverters.  I'm using Enphase 7A micro-inverters on each panel, so I also get direct data from enphase/enlighten about power production from each panel.
